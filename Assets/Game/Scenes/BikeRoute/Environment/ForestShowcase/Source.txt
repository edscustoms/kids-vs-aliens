Forest showcase understory assets

Source: the existing local Unity Terrain Tools Sample Asset Pack donor library
at Assets/TerrainSampleAssets. Its ThirdPartyNotices.txt states that the art
assets are governed by the standard Unity Asset Store EULA.

Reused meshes: Fern_A/B/C, Bush_A/B, Grass_A.
Reused textures: each family's BaseColor, Normal and MaskMap.
Meshes are copied without reshaping. Texture copies retain source pixels, use
new GUIDs and 1024px import limits. Mask G supplies URP ambient occlusion.
The three URP/Lit cutout materials and collider-free LOD prefabs are local
showcase assets; no dependency on the ignored donor folder remains.

Conifer trees separately reuse the project's existing BOTD URP assets.
